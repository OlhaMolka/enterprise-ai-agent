Fraud Prevention Policy

Department: Risk

This document defines fraud prevention and monitoring controls within the banking group.

Key fraud risks include:

* Internal employee fraud
* Payment manipulation
* Identity theft
* Unauthorized account access
* Vendor invoice fraud

The organization applies continuous transaction monitoring to identify suspicious activity patterns.

High-risk transactions must be escalated to the Fraud Investigation Team within 2 hours.

All fraud incidents are classified according to severity and financial impact.

Employees must complete mandatory fraud awareness training annually.

Critical fraud alerts are reviewed by the Operational Risk Committee on a weekly basis.

The bank uses behavioral analytics and anomaly detection mechanisms to identify unusual customer activity.

Third-party vendors handling payment data must comply with fraud prevention standards and pass annual security assessments.
